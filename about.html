About Me

I am an enthusiastic and detail-oriented ICT graduate from Maseno University with a strong interest in software development, web technologies, and IT systems. I hold a Bachelor's Degree in Information and Communication Technology.

I have technical skills in front-end and back-end development using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Java, Python, PHP, Go (Golang), Rust, and MySQL. I enjoy designing and developing responsive websites, dynamic web applications, and database-driven systems.

Beyond software development, I have experience and knowledge in computer networking, systems administration, hardware and software troubleshooting, technical support, and ICT training. I am passionate about solving real-world problems through technology and building reliable, secure, and user-friendly digital solutions.
